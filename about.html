Who I am

My name is Rashnie Hardin, and I was born on 24 March 2003. I am currently studying Software Engineering at the University of Applied Science and Technology Suriname (UNASAT). During my studies I have learned that technology is not only about writing code; it is also about understanding problems, people and how a digital solution can make something easier.

I am a curious and determined person who likes to keep learning. Sometimes a technical problem can feel challenging at first, but I have learned to approach it step by step, search for solutions and keep improving. That mindset is one of the qualities I want to carry with me into my professional career.

In the future, I would like to specialize further in technology. I am especially interested in areas where software, data and technology can be used to solve practical problems. I want to continue expanding my knowledge and discover which technical specialization fits me best.
